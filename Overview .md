1. Watch Sales Analysis

Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard focused on evaluating e-commerce watch sales performance, analyzing revenue streams, customer demographics, payment preferences, and regional distribution to drive profitability.

Key Insights
* Top Revenue Generator & Profit Drivers: Total sales reached ₹503K with a profit margin of 9.13%, where Madhya Pradesh led regional sales (₹124.6K) and Military Watches delivered the highest profit among sub-categories (₹12.5K).
* Payment Preference: Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred payment method, accounting for 45.89% of overall order volume (725 orders) and generating ₹179.4K in revenue.
* Target Audience Focus: Male customers represent the primary target segment, accounting for the highest order volume (1,000 orders) compared to Female and Unisex categories.

Tools Used
Power BI | Data Modeling | DAX | Drill through | Bookmarks | Report Page Tooltips | Interactive Slicers
